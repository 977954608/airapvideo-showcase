## Product capabilities

- **Two performers:** Start with two separate portraits or one clear photo of both people.
- **Six stages:** Orange Booth, Marble Lobby, Neon Studio, Rooftop Night, Graffiti Alley, and House Party.
- **Rap language:** English, Mandarin Chinese, and Japanese; Korean is available with Flagship.
- **Video controls:** Choose Standard, Pro, or Flagship and the supported resolution, duration, and aspect ratio. Available combinations depend on the selected model.
- **Topic direction:** Describe a birthday, shared memory, friendly celebration, or team milestone.
- **MP4 output:** Preview the completed result and download it from the website.

Audio is newly generated; the product does not clone the performers' real voices. Topic input guides generation and does not guarantee exact lyrics. AI appearance, timing, motion, and audio can vary between runs.

## How to use it

1. Open the website and choose two portraits or one duo photo.
2. Use photos you have permission to upload, with the agreement of both performers.
3. Pick a stage, topic, rap language, and video settings.
4. Sign in and review the displayed credit cost before submitting.
5. Inspect the finished video and download your MP4.

Generation uses credits. Check current pricing and purchase availability on the website; this repository does not promise free credits or a particular payment option. Save results promptly; the website currently states a 30-day result storage period.

## Examples and expectations

The website presents six sample generations with fictional performers, labeled Pro · 720p · 10s. These illustrate particular outputs, not a guarantee for every input. Watch the samples on the website with sound enabled before choosing settings.

This project does not claim artist endorsement, affiliation with a record label, access to an original commercial recording, or guaranteed commercial rights for generated outputs. Review your intended use and the rights to all inputs.
